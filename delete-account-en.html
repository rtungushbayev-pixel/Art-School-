Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your KasteyevSchool app account and the data linked to it. You can delete the account in the app, or request deletion if you no longer have the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the KasteyevSchool app and sign in.
  2. Go to the "Profile" tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete account".
  4. Confirm twice. The account is deleted immediately.

Delete without the app

Email the address below with the subject "Account deletion". Include the full name and the phone number or email you signed up with, and for a parent, the child's full name. Write from the email on the account, or be ready to confirm that the account is yours.

info@kasteyev.kz

We will delete the account and confirm within 10 business days.

What data is deleted

Deleted: your profile (name, photo, phone, email), posts, stories, comments, artwork photos, friends list and support requests.

Kept: class attendance records, for the periods required by the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan for educational records. Documents under the paid tuition agreement are kept separately from the app, under the terms of that agreement.

More details in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
